# Researched future additions — 2026-08-10

These Tier 1 candidates were identified during the 2026-08-10 moat research session. They are recorded for follow-up only: this dispatch does not add them to `datapulse.json` or make availability claims beyond the session findings.

## Candidate source families

- **DOSM Time-Series backfiles** — 22 series spanning 1963 to the present, published as XLS files at `dosm.gov.my/portal-main/time-series`.
- **Income Taxation dashboard** — a data.gov.my dashboard stewarded by LHDNM that is not represented in OpenDOSM.
- **KKMNOW full Ministry of Health catalogue** — at least 10 untracked datasets, including blood donations, organ pledges, Malaysian National Health Accounts (MNHA), and cosmetics data.
- **APAD land transport registry** — nine operator registries identified, with none currently tracked.
- **Hansard catalogue and search** — `hansard.parlimen.gov.my` Next.js data routes were probed without authentication and verified accessible on 2026-08-10.

## Follow-up rule

Before any candidate is added, re-verify its authoritative URL, licence, steward, schema, update cadence, historical coverage, and probe method. Dataset IDs and manifest entries should be proposed in a separate dispatch.
