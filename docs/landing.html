CLAIM BOUNDARIES

Evidence is not a universal truth verdict.

  • DataPulse is read-only; official publishers remain the source of record.
  • Integrity, provenance, freshness, and structural checks do not establish substantive truth.
  • Unknown, stale, degraded, inaccessible, and conflicting signals remain visible.
  • Agents and humans decide whether the available evidence is fit for a particular use.
