DataPulse-MY is the producer-side input-data trust layer for Malaysian public datasets, not a regulator or model evaluator. This inventory enumerates the contracts that already exist (live probes, signed receipts, MCP affordances) and the future contracts MY-AISafe and ASEAN AI SAFE will demand. Each row must answer: what claim is being made, by whom, against what evidence, with what verification path. The trust claim from MY-AISafe's perspective must be satisfiable today, before the regulator builds anything.

# MY-AISafe contract inventory

This is a factual inventory of producer-side observation and evidence contracts. DataPulse-MY makes claims about observed inputs and their evidence; it does not make regulatory, model-safety, semantic-truth, lineage-certification, or halal-certification claims.
