## Known quirks

- Official catalogue caveat: This data is derived based on births registered with JPN. Accordingly, if a birth is not registered with JPN (for instance, if a foreigner chooses to register their child in their home country, or if a resident in a remote area does not register the birth of their child), it will not count in this dataset. Furthermore, it should be noted that the number of administrative districts increased from 147 to 160 in 2021, due to the creation of new districts in Kelantan (1), Perak (1), Sabah (2), and Sarawak (9).
