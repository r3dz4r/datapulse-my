## Known quirks

- Official catalogue caveat: There are several caveats to note: - Values for W.P. Putrajaya are subsumed under values for W.P. Kuala Lumpur due to difficulty in separating the attribution of economic value. - Sectoral values of less than RM 5 million are intentionally nulled; these are different from values which are explicitly enumerated as 0. - The sum of GDP for all sectors may not always equal to the values for overall GDP because of independent rounding to one decimal place. However, the differences are not obvious.
