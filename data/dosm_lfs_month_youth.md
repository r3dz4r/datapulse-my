## Known quirks

- Official catalogue caveat: Data prior to 2025 is compiled in concordance with population data from the 2010 Census. Data from 2025 onwards is compiled in concordance with population data from the 2020 Census. As such, you may observe a trend break in the series due to the different references used. Furthermore, sums of each category may not always equal to the totals shown in related tables because of independent rounding to one decimal place. However, the differences are not obvious.
