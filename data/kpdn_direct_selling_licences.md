## Fields and known quirks

Each row includes `company_name`, `registration_no`, `ajl_no`, `status`, `status_raw`, `licence_start`, `licence_expiry`, `sector`, `address_management`, `address_operations`, `source_section`, `source_url`, and `fetched_at`. `raw` retains all published column text and `raw_cells` retains cells even when the column count is malformed; `issues` records parsing problems. Only AJL whitespace, surrounding field whitespace, status vocabulary and dates are normalised.

- The measured main-page dates are DD/MM/YYYY; DD.MM.YYYY (including single-digit day/month) is also supported. Raw strings remain available. A dash, blank or absent field becomes null; invalid dates become null with an issue.
- Sector and operations address are not published in these tables and remain null. PELAN on the historical cancellation page is retained in raw data and is not guessed to be a sector or split into Malay/English components.
- The expired and historical cancellation tables omit registration numbers. The historical cancellation page has no row status field: status is cancelled from its published page context, `status_raw` is null, and `status_context_raw` retains that context.
- 70 historical rows publish numeric-only licence numbers. They are retained without inventing an AJL prefix; overlap counts use only the normalised strings, without resolving these numbers to prefixed identifiers.
- Cancellation dates, plan and state remain in raw data; cancellation dates are never substituted for licence start or expiry.
- Published status is not recalculated from today's date and is not proof of current legal standing. Multiple observations of an AJL are not collapsed.
