## Known quirks

- Official catalogue caveat: Data on hardcore poverty is not available at DUN constituency level. Furthermore, DUN-level data is only published from 2019 (relative to [state-level data](https://open.dosm.gov.my/data-catalogue/hh_poverty_state) which is available from 1970) as prior HIES samples were not sufficient to support DUN-level granularity.
