DOSM and OpenDOSM publish through `storage.dosm.gov.my` and a `data.gov.my` mirror. The CSV `date` column is the observation period (the reference period of the statistic), not the publication date, so the probe rule must treat the date as observation-period semantics rather than a publisher publication timestamp.

The `dosm_hh_*` survey series (income, poverty, inequality) follow a biennial-to-triennial survey cadence, so an observation-period date of 2024 is expected and must not be reported as publisher retirement.
