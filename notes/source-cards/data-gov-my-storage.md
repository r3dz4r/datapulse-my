storage.data.gov.my serves point-in-time CSV snapshots for federal agencies. Unlike the OpenAPI catalogue, these are static files where the `date` column is the observation period; the HTTP Last-Modified header is unreliable, so freshness is read from the row date. A stale row date here means the publisher has stopped refreshing the file, not that the endpoint is down.

`ridership_headline` is the clearest false-positive trap: it declares a daily cadence but its date column has not advanced, and any 30-day or 90-day fallback would hide the fact that the publisher has stopped refreshing recent rows.
