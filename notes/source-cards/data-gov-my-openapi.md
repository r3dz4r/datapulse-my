The data.gov.my OpenAPI catalogue is the primary machine-readable surface for federal open data. HTTP success establishes availability only; the row `date` column establishes content freshness. The platform currently picks the row date as `content_freshness_date` even when the publisher has stopped or slowed a series (e.g. `covid_cases`, `vaxreg_covid`, `vaxreg_covid_demog`), so a stale row date must be interpreted as a publisher-likely-retired signal rather than an endpoint outage.

Four OpenAPI datasets are reference tables (`currency_codes`, `poskod`, `lookup_federal_finance`, `lookup_money_banking`) and carry `data_type=reference`; they have no freshness clock and must not be reclassified stale from row-date age alone.
